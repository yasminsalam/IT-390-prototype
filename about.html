WHY FOCUSOS?

Understand distraction. Build better focus.

FocusOS is designed to help students and remote workers become more aware of their attention during work sessions.

THE PROBLEM

Attention is easy to lose.

Phones, notifications, and other distractions can interrupt work sessions and reduce productivity.

THE SOLUTION

Accountability in real time.

FocusOS combines session goals, distraction monitoring, interventions, and productivity information in one system.

PERSONALIZATION

Every session has a purpose.

Users can identify what they plan to complete before starting so each session is connected to a specific goal.

PROGRESS

Review what happened.

The dashboard allows the user to review completed-session information once real data is provided by the backend.
